Stream Yours' — Free Peer-to-Peer Movie & Series Streaming Web App

Stream Yours' turns any modern web browser into a private, real-time media host. Share local movie files and multi-season TV series folders directly from your PC, Mac, or phone to any viewer worldwide over encrypted WebRTC DataChannels—with zero cloud uploads, zero file size caps, and instant embedded audio language switching.

Direct Browser-to-Browser P2P

Videos stay on your local storage and stream on demand in paced 192 KB binary frames with adaptive backpressure flow control, eliminating slow upload queues and third-party storage limits.

Automatic Movies & Series Grouping

Drop a folder once: standalone films appear in Movies with parsed resolution, year, and codec badges, while subfolders automatically organize into Series with episode playlists and Next/Prev controls.

Embedded Multi-Language Audio

Powered by MP4Box and HTML5 Media Source Extensions (MSE), the cinema player detects embedded ISO-639 audio tracks (English, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, and more) for instant mid-stream language switching.

Keyframe-Accurate Seeking & Resume

Scrub across multi-gigabyte 1080p and 4K files using exact HH:MM:SS timeline previews, double-tap ±10s fullscreen gestures, variable playback speeds (0.5x–2x), and automatic local resume progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Stream Yours' stream videos without uploading them to a server?

Stream Yours' establishes a direct browser-to-browser WebRTC DataChannel between the host device and the viewer. Video chunks are read locally on demand and streamed directly over P2P without ever storing your video files on a cloud server.

Is Stream Yours' free to use and does it have file size limits?

Yes, Stream Yours' is completely free to use in any modern browser. Because files remain on your local drive and stream in paced binary chunks with backpressure flow control, you can host large 1080p or 4K movie files and multi-season series folders.

How are Movies and TV Series organized automatically?

When you host a folder, standalone video files are categorized under Movies, while videos grouped inside subfolders are automatically detected as Series with numbered episode playlists and Next/Previous episode controls.

Can I switch embedded audio languages during P2P playback?

Yes. Stream Yours' uses an integrated MP4Box and Media Source Extensions engine to inspect embedded audio tracks (such as English, Hindi, Japanese, or Korean) and lets you switch languages mid-stream from the player header.

What happens when I close the tab or click Stop Hosting?

Clicking Stop or closing the host browser tab immediately removes your device's live presence entry and tears down active WebRTC sessions so your catalog disappears from viewer screens right away.

Who can see my files and my IP address?

Hosting publishes only your device label and the names and sizes of the videos you choose, so other visitors can browse them. Your video data is never uploaded anywhere. While a viewer is watching, your two browsers connect directly, which means each side can see the other's network address, as with any peer-to-peer connection. Only host files you have the right to share, and click Stop Hosting to remove your listing.

Which video formats and devices are supported?

Stream Yours' supports MP4, M4V, MOV, WebM, MKV, and other HTML5-compatible video containers across Windows, macOS, Linux, Android, and iOS browsers. Subtitle files (.srt or .vtt) kept next to a video are hosted with it and can be switched on from the CC button in the player.
